# Bundled fonts

Both families are free to redistribute alongside this site, under the licences
noted below.

- **Inter** — Rasmus Andersson. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/rsms/inter
  Body text, and every heading from `h3` down. Retrieved from the Fontsource
  distribution (Latin subset), unmodified.

- **Roboto** — Christian Robertson / Google. Apache License 2.0.
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto
  `h1` and `h2` only. Retrieved from Google Fonts (Latin subset), unmodified.

Newsreader (Production Type) was removed on 2026-08-17 when `h1`/`h2` changed
from serif to sans-serif. Nothing references it any more.

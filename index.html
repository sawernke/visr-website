Geospatial analysis and geophysical survey

The Vanderbilt Institute for Spatial Research (VISR) provides geospatial analysis and geophysical survey for diverse projects within Vanderbilt and for diverse clients in Nashville, middle Tennessee, and nationally.

About VISR

A core research facility at Vanderbilt

Our collaborations span the social sciences, humanities, medicine, public health, and epidemiology, STEM education, environmental health and science, and engineering.

VISR houses advanced geospatial computational resources, including high-spec workstations, servers, and commercial and open-source software tools, alongside geophysical and land survey instrumentation.

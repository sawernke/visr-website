Trainings

At VISR, we aim not only to provide geophysical and geospatial consulting services, but also to help educate students on working with our geophysical survey equipment. We are in the process of creating a series of educational videos with our partner lab, the Spatial Analysis Research Lab at Vanderbilt, explaining how to operate our equipment, along with documents to follow along with. Check back soon for more trainings to come!
